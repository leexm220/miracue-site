MiraCuePrivacy policy

ACCOUNT DELETION

Delete your MiraCue account

Delete in the app

Open Settings → Account → Delete account. Verify your identity using your password or Google account. MiraCue reports success after Firebase confirms that your sign-in account has been deleted.

Request deletion without the app

Email leexm550@gmail.com from your registered email address, using the subject “MiraCue account deletion request”. State that you want your MiraCue account and associated cloud account data deleted. Never send your password.

If you cannot access that email, contact the same support address and explain your situation. We may ask you to verify ownership before processing the request. Opening the email link does not send a request until you send the email; if no email app opens, copy the address into your email service. We will confirm when your request has been processed.

What is deleted and what remains

Deletion removes your MiraCue sign-in account and its email, profile and associated authentication data under Firebase's retention rules. Backup removal may take up to 180 days. MiraCue does not sync or back up your captures, favorites or personal pose templates.

Photos, favorites and templates stored on your phone remain local. Remove photos in Album and templates in My poses; uninstalling removes the app's private device data. Copies exported to your system gallery or shared elsewhere must be deleted at their destination.

注销咔位 / MiraCue 账号

在应用中打开“设置 → 账号 → 注销账号”，重新验证身份后注销。也可使用注册邮箱发送邮件至 leexm550@gmail.com，主题填写“MiraCue account deletion request”，明确申请删除账号及相关云端账号数据。请勿发送密码。

若无法访问原邮箱，请说明情况，我们可能需要核验账号所有权。点击邮箱链接不会自动发送或注销，发送请求后由开发者处理并确认。备份中的账号数据按 Firebase 规则清理，最长可能需要 180 天。本机照片、收藏和模板不上传，也不会随注销删除；可在应用内删除，已导出或分享的副本需在对应位置删除。
