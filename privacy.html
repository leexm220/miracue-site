PRIVACY POLICY

MiraCue Privacy Policy / 隐私说明

Effective August 14, 2026 · Account edition updated October 11, 2026

English

MiraCue is an Android camera app for pose guidance and photo editing. This policy covers optional accounts in overseas builds and the local camera features. Earlier releases and domestic builds without overseas sign-in do not send account data to Firebase.

1. Camera and device content

Camera frames are used after you enter the camera and grant permission, for preview, pose detection, framing and photos. MediaPipe and LiteRT process body keypoints, outlines, masks and matching scores on your device. Guidance uses an installed offline voice engine. Photos you select with the Android photo picker are used for personal templates or editing; the app does not browse your entire gallery.

Captured photos, thumbnails, personal templates, favorites, shoot records and camera preferences stay in app-private device storage. Signing in does not back up or sync them; the device library is shared across sign-ins. A copy reaches your system gallery only when you choose to export it. Sharing grants the selected recipient app access to the chosen photo, under that app's privacy policy.

2. Optional account service

Overseas sign-in uses Google Firebase Authentication for email/password and Google accounts. Firebase processes email, account profile (including a display name and basic Google profile where authorized), user identifiers, authentication credentials, IP addresses and client information for account management, security and abuse prevention in the United States. Email-account passwords are hashed by Firebase; Google sign-in does not disclose your Google password to MiraCue. We include no advertising, behavioral analytics, payment, cloud photo storage or remote pose inference service. Read Firebase's privacy and security information.

3. Startup choice and permissions

A privacy choice appears on first launch and after policy updates. Authentication and background photo processing wait for consent. You can use the camera, templates and album without an account. Camera permission is requested when you use the camera. Storage-write permission is requested only on Android 8/9 when exporting to your system gallery. Imported photos use the system picker without broad library permission. You can revoke permissions in Android settings or withdraw privacy consent in MiraCue settings; withdrawal signs you out and returns to the notice, without deleting your account or local content.

4. Retention and deletion

Account settings provide profile management, sign-out and account deletion with reauthentication. Success is shown only after Firebase confirms deletion. Firebase retains account information until deletion is initiated; removal from live and backup systems may take up to 180 days under its rules. See MiraCue account deletion to request deletion without the app.

Delete local photos and templates inside the app; uninstalling removes its private device data. Copies exported to the system gallery or shared elsewhere must be deleted there. Account deletion does not remove this device library.

5. Children and updates

MiraCue is not directed at children. If our features or data practices change, we will update this policy and provide a new in-app notice where needed.

6. Contact

Developer: an individual developer. Privacy, access, correction, deletion and support requests: leexm550@gmail.com.

简体中文

MiraCue / 咔位相机是一款安卓拍摄姿势引导与照片编辑应用。本说明涵盖海外版本的可选账号和本机拍摄功能；不含海外账号功能的旧版本与国内渠道不会向 Firebase 发送账号数据。

1. 本机拍摄与模板

相机仅在你进入拍摄并授权后用于取景、姿势识别、构图和拍照。MediaPipe、LiteRT 和离线语音在本机运行。照片通过 Android 系统选择器主动导入，应用不会读取整个相册。拍摄照片、缩略图、个人模板、收藏、记录和拍摄偏好保存在本机私有目录，不自动上传、备份或同步。本机内容不会随账号切换。仅主动导出时保存到系统相册；主动分享时授权给所选应用，适用其隐私政策。

2. 可选海外账号

海外版本使用 Google Firebase Authentication 提供邮箱密码及 Google 登录。在注册和登录过程中，认证服务在美国处理邮箱、账号资料（昵称及经授权的 Google 基本资料）、用户标识、认证凭证、IP 地址及用于安全防滥用的客户端信息。邮箱密码由 Firebase 哈希处理；Google 登录不向本应用提供 Google 密码。应用不含广告、行为分析、支付、云照片存储或远端姿势识别。详情参见 Firebase 隐私与安全说明。

3. 启动提示与权限

首次启动及隐私版本更新时征求同意。同意前不初始化认证或启动后台照片处理。免登录仍可拍摄和使用本机模板。相机权限在进入拍摄时申请；Android 8/9 的存储写入权限仅在导出系统相册时申请；导入使用系统照片选择器。你可在系统设置撤回权限，或在应用设置撤回隐私同意。撤回将退出登录并返回说明，不删除账号或本机内容。

4. 保留与删除

账号设置支持昵称、邮箱密码账户的密码及邮箱管理、退出与重新认证后注销。Firebase 确认删除后才显示成功。账号资料保留至发起删除，按 Firebase 规则从线上及备份中清理，最长可能需要 180 天。应用外可通过 账号注销网页 请求删除。本机照片、收藏、模板不会因账号注销删除，可在应用中移除；卸载会清除私有目录，系统相册或已分享副本需在对应位置删除。

5. 儿童、更新与联系

本产品不以儿童为主要目标受众。功能和数据处理变化时更新说明并按需重新提示。开发者为个人开发者；访问、更正、删除和支持请求请联系 leexm550@gmail.com。
